Relative available water: r = (θ − wilting point) / (field capacity − wilting point). The event is r < 0.30. Soil survey estimates cover 0–30 cm while the sensor samples near 20 cm: this is an approximation, not measured crop stress.

Study coverage

Historical US station measurements support this research. Operational reporting delays and transfer to AgriSense's Indian study areas were not tested.

Nested evaluation

For outer year Y: fit through Y−2, calibrate on Y−1, test on Y. Daily inner validation uses Y−4 and Y−3 with earlier fitting data. Held-out stations are excluded from fitting, tuning and calibration; their observed past is available for filtering.
